9. Quality bar for "world class"

Non-negotiables

  • Mobile LCP under 2.5s; images optimized, banners rebuilt as live text
  • Accessibility: WCAG AA contrast (pale blue on white needs checking)
  • Product and review schema; stars in search
  • Photo/video reviews app; subscriptions app
  • Email and SMS: welcome, abandoned cart, post-purchase, replenishment
  • Meta, TikTok, Google pixels verified
  • Policy pages: shipping, returns, privacy, terms, ingredient and safety
  • Consistent tokens: one palette, two fonts

10. Open items to settle before design

Decisions and fixes

  1. Fix "HOCI" to "HOCl" in the banners, or rebuild them as live text.
  2. Live Shopify description already softened (stability, competitor and eye claims). Still to fix in the banners and Word doc: "stable for months", "day 1 = day 90", "dirty secret of the industry", "calms redness and irritation", and the EPA/FDA facility wording.
  3. Set inventory (currently 0) and confirm real shipping threshold, guarantee, and price tiers; the $35, 30-day and multi-pack discounts here are placeholders.
  4. Decide how much to speak to Kayla (breakouts, pores). Current copy is Sandra-leaning. Avoid acne treatment claims.
  5. Start collecting real, unedited reviews and photos now.
  6. Decide: Shopify theme as the base (e.g. a flexible free or paid theme) vs fully custom sections.
